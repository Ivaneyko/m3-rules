# Writing: UI text, alt text, truncation and text size

Sources read: 2026-11-20 ([snapshot](../sources/SNAPSHOT.md)). Message wording for errors, snackbars and dialogs is in [feedback placement → writing the message](../patterns/feedback-placement.md#writing-the-message).

## UI text

- **Sentence case everywhere**: titles, headings, labels, menu items, navigation, app bars and buttons; only product and brand names keep their capitals ([style guide → use sentence case](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_style-guide.md#use-sentence-case)).
- **State the consequence** of an action in neutral, direct words, without alarming warnings ([explain consequences](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_style-guide.md#explain-consequences)); specific titles and headings help people scan ([use scannable words and formats](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_style-guide.md#use-scannable-words-and-formats)).
- **"You" and "your"**; never "my" and "your" together; avoid "we" and "I", except "I" for ownership in agreements ("I agree to the terms of service") ([pronouns](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_style-guide.md#pronouns)).
- **No full stop after a single sentence** in labels, tooltips, bulleted lists, dialog body text and link text; full stops for several sentences ([skip periods and unnecessary punctuation](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_style-guide.md#skip-periods-and-unnecessary-punctuation)).
- **Contractions** (don't), except "do not" for emphasis; serial commas; commas in numbers from 1,000 (not in addresses or years); no colons in headings ([use contractions](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_style-guide.md#use-contractions), [use serial commas](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_style-guide.md#use-serial-commas), [use commas for numbers](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_style-guide.md#use-commas-for-numbers-between-1000-and-1-million), [skip colons in headings](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_style-guide.md#skip-colons-in-headings)).
- **No ellipsis on a menu item or button that opens a dialog** or starts a process; ellipses only for work in progress or cut text, with no space before ([use ellipses sparingly](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_style-guide.md#use-ellipses-sparingly)).
- **Avoid em dashes**; an **en dash without spaces** for ranges (9 AM–Noon); no spaces around hyphens ([use dashes with caution](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_style-guide.md#use-dashes-with-caution), [use hyphens with care](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_style-guide.md#use-hyphens-with-care)).
- **"&"** only in headlines, table headers, navigation labels and buttons, and "and" there too when it fits ([skip ampersands in body text](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_style-guide.md#skip-ampersands-in-body-text)). Parentheses define terms, not asides ([use parentheses to define terms](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_style-guide.md#use-parentheses-to-define-terms)).
- **Spell words out**: no "e.g." or "etc." ("for example", "and more") ([use abbreviations sparingly](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_style-guide.md#use-abbreviations-sparingly)).

## Writing for translation

- **Short, simple sentences**: other languages run about **1.5×** longer than English ([global writing → use short, simple sentences](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_global-writing.md#use-short-simple-sentences)).
- Explain local references (places, names, currencies, temperatures, date formats) for translators; no abbreviations except common time ones ([use global examples](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_global-writing.md#use-global-examples--explain-local-references), [avoid abbreviations](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_global-writing.md#avoid-abbreviations)).
- Repeat the noun instead of "it"; start with "this" or "that" only when the noun follows; don't use one word both as noun and verb (filter, change) in one string; little jargon ([clarify pronouns](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_global-writing.md#clarify-pronouns), [clarify "this" and "that"](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_global-writing.md#clarify-this-and-that), [clarify ambiguities](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_global-writing.md#clarify-ambiguities), [reduce technical jargon](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_global-writing.md#reduce-technical-jargon)).

## Alt text

- An image whose removal loses nothing is **decorative**: `alt=""` ([alt text → when to use alt text](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_alt-text.md#when-to-use-alt-text)).
- Describe **the meaning in context**, not every detail (the item for sale, not what's around it); the same image gets different alt text in different places ([focus on the meaning of the image](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_alt-text.md#focus-on-the-meaning-of-the-image), [alt text is subjective](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_alt-text.md#alt-text-is-subjective)).
- **Short**; never start with "Image of"; never repeat the caption; use the same words as the nearby text ([keep it short](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_alt-text.md#keep-it-short), [don't start alt text with "image of"](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_alt-text.md#dont-start-alt-text-with-image-of), [context matters](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_alt-text.md#context-matters)).
- Name the kind of image when it helps (chart, map, screenshot, diagram). A **chart**: "summary of the data + why it's shown", its key takeaways, and a link to the data; prefer interactive charts for analysis ([when to describe the type of image](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_alt-text.md#when-to-describe-the-type-of-image), [charts and graphs](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_alt-text.md#charts-and-graphs)).
- An informative GIF or animation needs alt text, like a title for it; long video uses video description instead ([video and motion alt text](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_alt-text.md#video-and-motion-alt-text)).
- **Text inside an image** isn't read: put the essential part in the alt text ([writing → embedded text in images](../sources/m3/foundations_writing.md#embedded-text-in-images)). Essential parts of an informative image need **3:1** (large text) or **4.5:1** (small text) ([essential and non-essential elements](../sources/m3/foundations_writing.md#essential-and-non-essential-elements)).

Open: the alt text page recommends **140** characters at most; the writing page says **up to 125** ([keep it short](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_alt-text.md#keep-it-short), [alternative text](../sources/m3/foundations_writing.md#alternative-text-alt-text)).

## Truncation and text size

- **No content may be lost** when text is enlarged, spaced out or translated ([text truncation → requirements](../sources/m3/foundations_writing.md#requirements)).
- **Wrap** critical text, or let the component grow ([text wrapping](../sources/m3/foundations_writing.md#text-wrapping), [height and width of components](../sources/m3/foundations_writing.md#height-and-width-of-components)). **An ellipsis only when the full text is reachable** through a tooltip or link ([ellipses with hover or link](../sources/m3/foundations_writing.md#ellipses-with-hover-or-link)).
- **Support at least 200% text**: text and line height scale; padding and the spacing inside components stay at 1×; components without text (checkbox, radio, progress) don't change. Without an OS setting, offer multipliers (1.5×, 2×) ([text resizing → background](../sources/m3/foundations_writing.md#background-1)).
- For large text: grow containers, **stack** side-by-side components, let content **scroll vertically** (never in both directions), and in tight places (app bar, navigation bar and rail, fixed tabs) show the enlarged label in a **tooltip** on touch and hold ([methods](../sources/m3/foundations_writing.md#methods)).

## Notifications

- About the person, not the product; precise, timely, actionable and relevant; **easy to opt out of in context**; never ads ([notifications](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_notifications.md), [opting in and out](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_notifications.md#opting-in-and-out)).
- The important part **first**; a clear, short call to action; **weekday names, not "today" or "tomorrow"**; don't repeat the product name; little dynamic text; emoji sparingly and never on bad news ([put important information at the top](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_notifications.md#put-important-information-at-the-top), [be day-specific](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_notifications.md#be-day-specific), [don't name the product (again)](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_notifications.md#dont-name-the-product-again), [avoid dynamic text](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_notifications.md#avoid-dynamic-text), [emoji with caution](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_notifications.md#emoji-with-caution)).
- Lengths: title under 29 characters, collapsed body under 40, expanded under 80; one or two buttons of one or two words; SMS under 160 characters in Latin languages, 134 in others ([mind your characters](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_notifications.md#mind-your-characters), [SMS messages](../sources/m3/foundations_content-design_notifications.md#sms-messages)).
